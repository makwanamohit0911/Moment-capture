FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book my session? +
We recommend booking 4–8 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date and time. For peak periods (spring and holiday season), we suggest booking 3 months ahead. Newborn sessions can be pre-booked during pregnancy.
What is included in a standard session? +
All sessions include a pre-session consultation, professional styling guidance, your selected session duration (typically 1–3 hours), full professional editing of all images, and delivery via our private online gallery within 2–3 weeks.
What should we wear to our session? +
We provide a full styling guide after booking. Generally, we recommend neutral, soft tones with complementary colours across your group. Avoid large logos or very busy patterns. Our team is happy to assist with outfit coordination during your consultation.
How many images will I receive? +
This depends on your package. Our standard sessions deliver 30–50 fully edited high-resolution images. Premium packages include 60–80+ images. All images are delivered in both full-resolution (for printing) and web-optimised formats.
Do you offer print products and albums? +
Yes. We offer a curated collection of fine art prints, heirloom albums, framed prints, and canvas artwork through our professional print laboratory partners. Our team will guide you through product selection after your gallery is delivered.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Sessions cancelled 48+ hours in advance receive a full credit towards rescheduling. We understand that families with young children face unpredictable schedules and are happy to accommodate rescheduling with reasonable notice at no extra charge.
